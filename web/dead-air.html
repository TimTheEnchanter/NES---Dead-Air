What the numbers mean

Frame rate counts finished game frames against the console's 60 Hz clock, so it reflects how the ROM performs, not your computer. Late frames took more than two vblanks.

This emulator's timing differs from Mesen's, so its frame rate can read higher or lower, and it draws the picture one line lower. Use it to try the game and catch visible faults. Use Mesen or a real console for final timing.

Mesen reference

The missions ran at about 25 fps in scripted flights, with zero picture slips across the timing range. The carrier and freighter close-ups held at 20 fps or better. These numbers come from an emulator, not real hardware.

How to play

Start opens the campaign. Press Fire to advance the text, and use up or down to pick your answer.

Fly to the bright dot, which marks the nav point. If it sits at the side of the view, turn that way. Hold B and press up or down to change speed. Hale calls out what you find at each point. In Mission 1 you meet two scouts. In Mission 2 you meet scouts and then a fleeing convoy of two big freighters, which take six hits each. Catch them within about 45 seconds or they jump away. In Mission 3 you destroy a relay buoy, then listen to the radio: someone warns you before trouble arrives. Mission 4 depends on your answer to Ren: escort her unarmed shuttle and hold off scouts for 90 seconds, or fly with Mara against the heavy lancer, whose bolts cost two shields, and destroy the listening post. In Mission 5 a scout gets on Hale's tail: kill it within about 30 seconds or he is lost for good. Then a strange ship appears, drawn in cyan. It never fires. When it stops at its last hit, you choose whether to finish it. In Mission 6 you guard the wreck of the Ashgrove for about two minutes while Ren copies its records. Enemy bolts damage the wreck, so go after whoever is shooting at it. Keep turning to dodge bolts. When the radio says to take us home, follow the marker to the Vigil and fly close to land. A crash or a bolt costs a shield, with a short grace period after each bump, but losing them all does not end the story. Your progress saves after each mission's last scene.

Sound

Sound starts off because browsers need a click first. Choose Sound off to turn it on. Effects and the title music use the console's real sound channels.
