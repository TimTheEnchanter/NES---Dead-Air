What the numbers mean

Frame rate counts finished game frames against the console's 60 Hz clock, so it reflects how the ROM performs, not your computer. Late frames took more than two vblanks.

This emulator's timing differs from Mesen's, so its frame rate can read higher or lower, and it draws the picture one line lower. Use it to try the game and catch visible faults. Use Mesen or a real console for final timing.

Mesen reference

The missions ran at about 25 fps in scripted flights, with zero picture slips across the timing range. The carrier and freighter close-ups held at 20 fps or better. These numbers come from an emulator, not real hardware.

How to play

Start opens the campaign. Press Fire to advance the text, and use up or down to pick your answer.

Fly to the bright dot, which marks the nav point. If it sits at the side of the view, turn that way. Hold B and press up or down to change speed. Hale calls out what you find at each point. In Mission 1 you meet two scouts. In Mission 2 you meet scouts and then two big freighters, which take six hits. Keep turning to dodge bolts. When the radio says to take us home, follow the marker to the Vigil and fly close to land. A crash or a bolt costs a shield, but losing them all does not end the story. Your progress saves after each debrief.

Sound

Sound starts off because browsers need a click first. Choose Sound off to turn it on. Effects and the title music use the console's real sound channels.
